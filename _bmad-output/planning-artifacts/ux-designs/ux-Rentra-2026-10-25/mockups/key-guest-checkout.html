Preview scope and other checkout states

Inputs, acceptance and disclosures are local UI examples. Values reset on navigation. Production retains session restoration, quote revalidation, explicit terms acceptance, holds, deadlines, hosted checkout and recovery.

  • Waiting for owner approval: payment is unavailable until approval; approval is not booking confirmation.
  • Price or policy changed: show the revised quote and obtain fresh acceptance before payment.
  • Hold expired or outcome unknown: recover the recorded payment status before inviting another charge.
  • Verified payment failure: offer retry only while current inventory, hold and payment rules allow it.
  • Late capture without inventory: show resolution/refund handling; never claim the stay is confirmed.

Gateway availability, taxes and live collection remain governed by their existing configuration and release gates. Test examples do not enable commercial payments.

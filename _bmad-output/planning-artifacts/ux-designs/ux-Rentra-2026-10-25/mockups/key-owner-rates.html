Preview scope and planned additions

Inputs and disclosures can be explored here. Changes do not persist; no review, publication, calendar or pricing service is called.

Approved richer child-age, bedding, facilities and configurable cancellation policies are planned requirements. They are not represented as delivered controls in this specimen.

Production must preserve validation, version conflicts, current save behavior, unsaved-change protection, and review warnings. The Rooms and Rates steps currently use explicit server saves; general draft-recovery code is not a guarantee of autosave for every hotel editor.

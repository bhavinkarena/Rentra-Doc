# Decision register reconciliation

Source: [market questions and answers](../../prds/prd-Rentra-2026-10-25/market-questions-and-answers.md), with the approved PRD/addendum controlling later decisions.

| Register theme | UX disposition |
|---|---|
| Optional property selection, suitable stays and mandatory known charges | Search/filters, room comparison and selected-trip total patterns. |
| Independent verification, publication, bookability and operational restrictions | IA, Review/Selection/Money profiles and admin evidence reference. |
| Honest support intake and review eligibility | Guest support/review flows, non-photo evidence and absence of automatic cancellation/refund from issue filing. |
| Normal 72h discovery reporting; urgent access/safety; 12-elapsed-hour human first response | Inherited Timing Contracts and urgent support example; staffing and resolution remain separate. |
| 30-day review window/day-7 reminder and owner correction reminders | Inherited Timing Contracts; no owner review veto or new unapproved reminder schedule. |
| Recovery, guest money and payout separation | UJ-4, receipt-clock rules and historical allocation reference. |

Adopted decisions are preserved; partial/evidence-gate rows remain implementation/operational dependencies. No additional product choice is inferred from a specimen.

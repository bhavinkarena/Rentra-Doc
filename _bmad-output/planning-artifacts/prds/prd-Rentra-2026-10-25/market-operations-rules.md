# Rentra operations: market answers to remaining questions

> Research snapshot: later founder approvals and overrides are recorded in the [current question-and-answer register](market-questions-and-answers.md) and [PRD](prd.md). Pending/open wording below describes the research stage, not necessarily the current decision.

Researched 2026-10-25 against the current PRD and reviewer findings. This is a research/input artifact, not an application audit. The founder authorizes using market practice to answer routine questions. Confirmed Rentra rules take precedence over competitor differences.

**Evidence labels:** **Market** = directly supported by cited primary practice/standard; **Synthesis** = recommended Rentra implementation/default inferred from that practice, not a universal market rule; **Unsettled** = needs Rentra capability, measurement, contractual or specialist evidence. No source establishes that Rentra already implements any answer.

## Publication, verification and availability

| ID | Question | Answer to adopt and evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OP-1 | Must owner verification finish before the property is public? | **Market:** yes as the publication gate: Airbnb requires hosts to finish identity verification before their listing is published. **Synthesis:** retain Rentra private drafts while pending; permit preparing/submitting evidence for team review, but approval/publication requires verified owner identity. Do not prevent draft progress merely to copy a publication gate. [Identity verification](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1237). |
| OP-2 | What does complete publication setup mean? | **Market:** Booking.com validates minimum content before Open/bookable: property/location, timing, units, contact, cancellation policy, photos, room/rate and priced availability, plus applicable contract/licence requirements. **Synthesis:** Rentra checklist includes verified owner, property evidence/review approval, complete address/map, contact, accurate category/photos, capacity/bed details, cancellation/house/child/pet policy as applicable, check-in/out, valid priced inventory and no blocking restriction. Hotels also require sellable room types, quantities, rate plans/meal disclosures; apartments require exclusive-unit setup/min-max nights. No blank required price or missing policy can publish. Do not import Booking.com-specific Latin-character rules, licence IDs or kitchen requirements blindly. [Opening requirements](https://developers.booking.com/connectivity/docs/property-api/status-endpoint/status-introduction). |
| OP-3 | Should publication require every future date to have availability? | **Market:** Booking.com requires priced inventory for at least one future date, not all dates. **Synthesis:** require a valid future sellable date/period and complete rates for offered inventory; owner may intentionally block others. A currently active property can still be unavailable for a selected date. No invented six/twelve-month inventory obligation. [Opening requirements](https://developers.booking.com/connectivity/docs/property-api/status-endpoint/status-introduction). |
| OP-4 | Can edits invalidate verification? | **Market:** Airbnb may reverify identity after name/account changes and blocks new bookings while host reverification is incomplete; changing a booked listing address is constrained. **Synthesis:** send owner identity, ownership/authority, address/location and materially different unit changes for recheck, preserving audit history and existing booking snapshots. Do not let an approved badge automatically certify replacement documents/location. Routine price/calendar edits follow owner controls and do not by themselves require identity review. Exact risk-field classification is an internal checklist. [Identity](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1237), [address changes](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/478). |
| OP-5 | Does verification prove legal ownership or property quality? | **Market:** Airbnb explicitly does not guarantee identity merely from verification. **Synthesis:** Rentra badge names what was checked; document review, video review and physical visit are separate recorded facts. Applicable proof-of-authority documents require local specialist/operations validation. [Identity](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1237). |
| OP-6 | When should team request corrections versus reject? | **Synthesis:** missing/correctable information gets itemized correction requests; clear invalidity, misrepresentation or inability to establish authority can be rejected with reason. Preserve founder permission for direct rejection, correction/resubmission and full logs. Markets do not determine Rentra documentary sufficiency or fraud appeal criteria; owner/operations checklist remains necessary, not a new prompt about each form field. |
| OP-7 | Does unlisting cancel existing stays? | **Market:** Airbnb unlisting stops new reservations/search visibility while confirmed stays must still be hosted. **Adopt:** Rentra stop-new-bookings option preserves confirmed stays. Preserve separately approved Rentra explicit cancel-upcoming option/full refunds, rather than removing it to mimic Airbnb. [Unlisting](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/476). |
| OP-8 | What counts as an upcoming stay for the bulk option? | **Synthesis:** scheduled check-in is strictly in the future in the booked property timezone; in-progress, completed and cancelled stays are excluded. At exact check-in, do not include in the upcoming batch. This is a deterministic boundary, not a published universal marketplace definition. Individual current-stay incidents go through support review. |
| OP-9 | What happens to pending/unpaid requests during deactivation? | **Market:** pending requests and unpaid reservations can hold dates; platform handling differs. **Synthesis:** show pending requests separately in deactivation preview. Stop accepting new requests/approvals after deactivation; retain legitimate existing holds until decline/expiry in stop-new-only mode, and require reactivation before an unpaid request can advance to confirmed booking. Explicit cancel-upcoming mode cancels applicable unpaid requests too and releases their holds; no refund on uncaptured money. Recheck active eligibility at capture and route raced captures to resolution/refund. Do not claim this exact behavior is Airbnb's. [Blocked calendars](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3612). This needs architecture acceptance cases, not a founder arithmetic quiz. |
| OP-10 | Should market timing replace Rentra's existing request deadlines? | **Adopt existing Rentra baseline:** up to24h owner response and12h approved payment, capped by arrival, plus release on expiry/decline as source documented. Airbnb uses24h response and different payment/expired-calendar behavior. Do not silently substitute its hold rules. [Airbnb calendar](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3612). |
| OP-11 | Must notices distinguish stop-new-only from cancellations? | **Synthesis:** yes: owner confirmation shows affected listing/bookings and individual results; affected guests receive cancellation/refund status and support route through portal+email; no cancellation notice for unchanged stays. Bulk action is never all-success if individual records fail. Channel duplication and retry deduplication are operational defaults, not an identical competitor specification. |

## Fees, discovery and quotation

| ID | Question | Answer to adopt and evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OP-12 | What scope should an owner-specific override have? | **Synthesis from explicit founder scope:** all properties belonging to that selected owner; otherwise the owner-level override is ambiguous. Admin with finance-config permission only, audit previous/new value, effective time and reason. No invented property/category override or owner self-editing. The percentage itself cannot be sourced as Rentra's optimal rate. |
| OP-13 | Do fee edits reprice already paid bookings? | **Synthesis:** no; preserve accepted booked fee/allocation. New quotes use effective configuration. A not-yet-paid quote that expires or becomes invalid must refresh and require guest review of any difference. Do not collect silently or mutate existing settled amounts. This is consistency with accepted pricing, not a claim all platforms use a specific quote TTL. |
| OP-14 | Should search show nightly bait price or trip total? | **Market:** Airbnb displays fee-inclusive trip totals in search globally, with tax-display differences by jurisdiction. **Adopt:** show selected-date/party trip total prominently with mandatory known charges; show taxes included where known and clear tax status/breakdown; disclose final payable total before full payment. Never hide Rentra/pet/required-bedding fees until after selection or call incomplete room-only price the payable total. [Global total-price display](https://news.airbnb.com/total-price-display-is-now-standard-globally), [tax display example](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3610). This does not decide India tax accounting. |
| OP-15 | How should new hotel/child/pet/apartment constraints affect results? | **Synthesis:** search eligible active/published inventory for dates and actual party; exclude unbookable capacity/pet/stay-length combinations, or clearly label a nonbookable alternative without letting it checkout. Hotels must return an available allocation/offer for the declared party, not just any room. Apartments reserve one exclusive unit. Quote is recalculated authoritatively before payment, with visible changes and recovery path. Existing booking eligibility must still be verified server-side. |
| OP-16 | What happens through login/navigation? | **Synthesis preserving FR24:** retain valid location/dates/party/room/pet selections through authentication; obtain fresh quote after expiry or changes. Show why selection is invalid rather than silently choosing different dates/room/party. No new library/session implementation is prescribed. |

## Support, contact and reviews

| ID | Question | Answer to adopt and evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OP-17 | When should guests see owner phone/exact address? | **Market:** Airbnb reveals phone numbers after confirmation; booked address is exact. **Adopt:** public discovery shows sufficient area/location details; expose private owner contact and exact private-home address to the authorized confirmed guest. Hotel public business address can remain public. Preserve portal request/contact path; do not add unsolicited off-platform payments. [Host contact](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/88), [contact before booking](https://www.airbnb.co.uk/help/article/147), [address](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/478). |
| OP-18 | What should a property/access complaint contain? | **Market:** Airbnb considers photos/video, host confirmation, severity, affected stay portion and whether guest vacates; it normally seeks host response. **Adopt:** booking link, issue category/description, discovery time, affected room/nights, desired remedy, relevant media/messages and owner response when feasible. Not all issues permit photos or waiting for owner; admin may review other credible evidence. Allegation is not automatic full-refund proof. [Issue refund policy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2868). |
| OP-19 | Is there a normal reporting deadline? | **Market:** Airbnb uses72h from issue discovery. **Adopt:** prompt report, preferably immediately for access/safety;72h discovery-based service-policy target, with admin review for documented inability/exception and without purporting to waive statutory rights. Record discovery and submission times. This is chosen Airbnb-based practice, not an industry-wide limitation period. [Issue refund policy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2868). |
| OP-20 | Should issue refunds depend solely on ordinary cancellation policy? | **Market:** Airbnb issue remedy can override ordinary cancellation; considers severity/evidence/affected portion. **Adopt:** ordinary cancellation follows accepted owner policy; verified property/access issues follow FR20 reviewed0?100 remedy and selected responsibility. Preserve Rentra no-owner-offset rule despite Airbnb's different owner settlement behavior. No automatic rebooking inventory or guarantee added. [Issue refund policy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2868). |
| OP-21 | Can Rentra promise24/7 urgent support or a fixed response SLA? | **Unsettled:** major-platform staffing does not establish founder+1?2 teammates' coverage. Classify denied access, safety and currently stranded guests as urgent; alert/escalate to duty team and expose truthful contact/coverage. Founder must supply actual hours/on-call arrangement and realistic acknowledgement target before launch. Do not invent a guaranteed15minute response or24/7 team. |
| OP-22 | How long should guests have to review? | **Market variation:** Airbnb homes14days; Booking.com may accept within3months. No universal answer. **Synthesis default:** preserve existing Rentra30day review window to avoid needless brownfield change; this lies between sourced marketplace windows but is an internal default, not a market rule. Define from booked checkout in property timezone; reviewed arrival-denial exceptions get the equivalent window from case-linked stay endpoint. Founder-authorized batch research permits this default without another multiple-choice question. [Airbnb window](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/995), [Booking standards](https://m.booking.com/content-moderation-policy/guest-reviews-standards.en-gb.html). |
| OP-23 | When should review invitations/reminders go? | **Market:** Airbnb sends invitation on checkout day. **Adopt:** invite eligible guests after checkout; stop reminders once submitted/closed/ineligible. **Synthesis:** one reminder day7, using the30day window; exact day7 is an internal low-volume default, not a claim about competitor cadence. No reward tied to positive rating. [Airbnb window](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/995), [review standards](https://www.booking.cn/content-moderation-policy/guidelines-and-standards-for-reviews.en-gb.html). |
| OP-24 | What basic review checks are appropriate without adopting ML? | **Market:** genuine experience, travel relevance, no privacy leaks, hate/threats, spam, manipulation or commercial solicitation. **Adopt:** eligibility/ownership/duplicate check plus content rules; ordinary compliant content auto-publishes, uncertain/flagged/reported content goes to admin. Implementations may be simple checks+manual queue; market ML is not scope. Negative rating alone remains permitted. [Booking standards](https://m.booking.com/content-moderation-policy/guest-reviews-standards.en-gb.html), [content guidelines](https://www.booking.cn/content-moderation-policy/guidelines-and-standards-for-reviews.en-gb.html). |
| OP-25 | Can owners respond to reviews or report them? | **Market:** Booking.com permits accommodation replies; Airbnb permits public responses and policy-based removal requests. **Adopt:** report/dispute to admin with reason/evidence; owners cannot delete, veto or rewrite guest reviews. Public owner replies are a supported market option, not essential new launch scope: preserve if already supported, otherwise defer rather than adding a new feature solely from research. If exposed, apply the same content/privacy rules. No reciprocal host review system or open-ended discussion thread implied. [Airbnb responses](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/32), [Booking standards](https://m.booking.com/content-moderation-policy/guest-reviews-standards.en-gb.html). |
| OP-26 | How long should identity documents/support evidence be retained? | **Unsettled:** a competitor's privacy policy does not determine Rentra's legal purpose/retention period. Private least-privilege access, secure storage, deletion/retention controls and audit are baseline requirements, but exact periods and lawful basis need India-specific specialist assessment and provider terms. Do not invent a7year or90day period. |
| OP-27 | Should correction alerts be portal, email or both? | **Synthesis:** both admin portal and transactional email; portal is durable source of request state, email points to it. Keep founder7/15/30 schedule and explicit resubmit alert. Delivery attempts/outcome recorded; email failure does not mark owner response or close request. This resolves A-1 as an adopted operational default under batch authorization, not a proven identical competitor channel policy. |

## Pets and assistance animals

| ID | Question | Answer to adopt and evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OP-28 | Can pets stay free? | **Market:** Airbnb makes pet fee optional and supports four units matching Rentra choices. **Adopt:** allowed pets can be free (zero charge), otherwise one configured fee unit per stay/night/pet/pet-night; show resulting total before payment and preserve it. No mandatory pet charge or price imported. [Pet fee](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3623). |
| OP-29 | Which restrictions are appropriate? | **Market:** Airbnb records pet count and house rules. **Synthesis:** owner sets maximum count and clearly disclosed permitted animal types/size or other reasonable property restrictions, plus stay rules; validate declared pet eligibility before payment. No allowed-on-request branch, hidden arrival surcharge or arbitrary platform maximum imported from Airbnb0?5. Generic all-animals restrictions cannot override a separate assistance policy. [Pet hosting](https://www.airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/what-you-need-to-know-about-hosting-pets-463), [pet fee](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3623). |
| OP-30 | Does pet policy apply only to hotels? | **Synthesis:** use the same allowed/not-allowed declaration and pricing model across Rentra's four accommodation types, with hotel applicability per offered room where relevant. Market examples span homes/hotels, but no source settles exact Rentra inheritance; use property default with room restrictions clearly disclosed rather than invent a pet-approval system. No extra category is added. |
| OP-31 | Are assistance animals ordinary pets and what are India's exact rules? | **Market:** Airbnb has a separate service-animal accessibility policy, generally no pet fees/refusal, with policy exceptions and jurisdiction qualifications. **Adopt principle:** keep assistance cases separate; do not automatically block/charge under ordinary pet rules. **Unsettled India legal detail:** reviewed India Code RPwD Act and tourism accessibility draft establish a relevant nondiscrimination/accessibility framework, but did not establish an exact operational assistance-animal documentation/exemption/fee test for every Rentra accommodation. Specialist validation required; do not import US ADA definitions, two-question test or emotional-support rules as Indian law. [Airbnb accessibility policy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1869), [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2155?locale=en), [Tourism draft?explicitly draft](https://tourism.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-06/Notice%20%26%20Draft%20version%20of%20Accessible%20Tourism%20Guidelines.pdf). |

## Remaining questions the market cannot answer for Rentra

1. What real urgent-support hours/on-call arrangement and acknowledgement target can the founder's team deliver? Do not promise24/7 merely because a major platform has it.
2. What exact evidence retention/deletion periods, lawful processing basis and document sufficiency apply to Rentra? Specialist/provider review, not a founder guess.
3. What India-specific assistance-animal accommodation, exemption and documentation rules apply to the actual properties? Specialist validation remains necessary. A no-pets toggle is insufficient evidence.
4. What measured deployment/load budget and supported device/browser matrix can Rentra pass? Engineering/UX evidence, not a market answer.
5. What actual global fee percentage, launch supply/customer plan and refund funding capacity does Rentra choose? Preserve unresolved budget/rate decisions for the finance/product research owner; do not invent them.

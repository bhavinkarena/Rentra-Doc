# Rentra: all remaining questions and researched answers

Research and adoption date: 2026-10-25. This is the complete collected question register from the PRD and both reviews: **83 research entries**, including overlapping checks and previously confirmed answers, not 83 new founder questions. Compatible answers are adopted under the founder instruction to use researched defaults. This does not authorize code, deployment, contacting owners or moving money.

**Precedence:** explicit founder decisions win over competitor differences. Market facts and Rentra product/engineering synthesis are distinguished in the three evidence reports. No claim is made that one universal global flow exists. Earlier review/reconciliation reports remain dated snapshots; this register and updated PRD record their resolution.

**Status:** Adopted = answered by existing decision, compatible market practice or clearly labelled Rentra synthesis. Open = real business choice still needed. Validate = provider, legal, operational or measured evidence needed; these are not questions a founder opinion can certify. Partial = routine rule adopted but a stated boundary/gate remains.

## Business choices that market research cannot make

1. **Early departure - answered:** owner controls availability/time-based terms; if offered, apply percentage only to unused nights and attributable components. Used nights excluded ordinarily, property issues reviewed (ST-2/ST-3).
2. **Refund liquidity and exposure:** INR 40,000 proposed for assessment, not confirmed funding. Validate adequacy and agree exposure limits/monitoring (M-20/OQ-2).
3. **Global fee - answered:** approved 5% launch default with owner overrides; review actual margin after first five paid bookings, not an automatic rate increase (M-20/OQ-9).
4. **Standard gateway costs - answered:** Rentra pays from its service fee; supersedes shared costs, with no ordinary owner gateway deduction or additional guest processing surcharge (M-18/OQ-3).
5. **Successive owner-funded awards - commercially answered:** founder approves audited retrospective cumulative liability including above original proceeds; clear owner consent and specialist validation remain (M-7/OQ-3/OQ-5).
6. **Owner repayment notice - answered:** confirmed delivery or authenticated view/acknowledgement starts the clock; otherwise admin confirms receipt. Channel evidence still needs implementation validation (M-12/OQ-10).
7. **Real support coverage:** initial response within 12 elapsed hours confirmed; actual coverage, on-call owner and urgent escalation remain (OP-21/OQ-11).

## Evidence gates, not more opinion questions

- Provider approval and test evidence for capture, early payout, partial/old-payment refunds, repayment links, reconciliation and disputes.
- Indian tax/invoice/remittance and owner contract review; evidence retention/document sufficiency and assistance-animal handling.
- Engineering/UX measurements for concurrency, browser/device coverage and quality targets; supported apartment duration/booking lead time, owner check-in eligibility and cot inventory. No universal 28-night platform cap is adopted.
- Real owner acquisition and guest interviews; launch city remains supply-dependent. Market research cannot prove owner willingness, demand or a million-dollar outcome.

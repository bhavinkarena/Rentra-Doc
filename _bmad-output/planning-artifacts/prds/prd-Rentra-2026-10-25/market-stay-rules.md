# Market answers: stay, child and apartment rules

> Research snapshot: later founder approvals and overrides are recorded in the [current question-and-answer register](market-questions-and-answers.md) and [PRD](prd.md). Pending/open wording below describes the research stage, not necessarily the current decision.

Research date: 2026-10-25. Input: current `prd.md`, `review-rubric.md` and `review-money-flow.md`. User authorizes market-informed answers instead of serial minor questions. This document proposes Rentra defaults; it does not claim one worldwide standard, change existing founder decisions, certify legal compliance or prove implementation.

## Source findings

- **S1 ? [Airbnb cancellation policies](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/475):** short stays and stays of 28+ nights have separate policies. After-check-in outcomes vary: flexible/moderate rules distinguish stayed and unspent nights, while other policies may provide no refund. Listing timezone matters. Airbnb's fees/taxes and grace periods must not replace Rentra's confirmed full-paid-percentage model.
- **S2 ? [Booking.com cancellation policies](https://developers.booking.com/connectivity/docs/policies-api/understanding-cancellation-policy):** property-specific cancellation and no-show conditions can use percentages or nights. Rentra already chose percentages, so night-penalty configuration is not imported.
- **S3 ? [Airbnb discretionary refund requests](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1370):** personal circumstances remain subject to the booked cancellation policy unless an exception is agreed; resolution requests can seek partial/full refunds. Its 60-day request window is a platform choice, not a global deadline or Rentra requirement.
- **S4 ? [Airbnb issue evidence](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2993):** cleanliness complaints should be documented and reported promptly; its example window is 72 hours from discovery. Remedies depend on severity. This is evidence for an issue-review path, not a reason to automatically refund unsupported claims.
- **S5 ? [Booking.com child policies](https://developers.booking.com/demand/docs/accommodations/child-policies):** actual ages are retained; child rates, admission restrictions, occupancy and cot/bed policies are separate. A pricing treatment does not change age. Property/room limits and current price need validation for the actual party.
- **S6 ? [Agoda child, bed and occupancy setup](https://partnerhub.agoda.com/how-do-i-set-up-room-occupancy-child-rate-and-age-groups-and-extra-beds/):** age groups can have separate rates; property defaults and rate-plan customizations exist. Child accommodation, meals, extra beds and cots are distinct. Percentage-of-adult pricing divides room price by searched adult count. Its room setup ensures an adult is present. Cot handling may be on request rather than guaranteed with booking.
- **S7 ? [Agoda booking occupancy API](https://developer.pps.agoda.com/docs/api-reference):** each room requires at least one adult; child ages are integers from 0 to 17. This supplies a usable launch input model, not legal-adulthood advice.
- **S8 ? [Airbnb trip lengths](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/880):** owners control minimum/maximum nights and can vary minimum stays by check-in day. It does not establish a universal Rentra maximum.
- **S9 ? [Airbnb availability controls](https://www.airbnb.com.au/resources/hosting-homes/a/updating-your-availability-708):** owner preparation-time options include none, one or two nights around reservations. This supports configurable turnover time rather than compulsory empty nights for every home.
- **S10 ? [Airbnb cleaning fees](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2812):** cleaning is a host-set one-off component of total price. Its current refund behavior depends on policy/timing; older unconditional-before-check-in descriptions are stale. Rentra's already-confirmed full-paid refund percentages take priority.
- **S11 ? [Booking.com occupancy examples](https://developers.booking.com/demand/docs/accommodations/occupancy-use-cases):** actual guest allocation and property age restrictions must be checked; child-rate information alone cannot decide physical room eligibility.

## Questions market evidence cannot settle

1. **Voluntary early-departure basis conflict (ST-2):** applying percentages only to unused nights is a sensible market model, but it changes/qualifies the previously approved percentage-of-full-booking payment. Founder confirmation is warranted unless the parent treats the new market-default authorization as permission to adopt this explicit exception. State the exception prominently either way; do not silently deduct consumed nights from an ordinary pre-arrival cancellation.
2. **Maximum apartment duration and local boundary (AP-3):** no universal global number. Owner max must stay within supported inventory/provider behavior and applicable local conditions. A 27-night launch cap would be a new Rentra cut, not a fact dictated by Airbnb.
3. **Age/adult eligibility:** source API adult counts are not a substitute for Rentra's legally/operationally validated minimum check-in age and policy for families with children in connecting/separate rooms. Preserve actual ages; do not label a 13-year-old a legal adult because of pricing.
4. **Cot commercial details:** market proves both on-request and configured policies, not Rentra's inventory accuracy or team capacity. Owners need to provide genuine quantities/prices; unsupported required cot promises block valid checkout. No new later-payment exception is authorized.
5. **Numerical restrictions, deadlines and prices:** no market source supplies the correct Rentra fee amounts, maximum child bands/guests, standard child ages, all pricing precision rules, incident reporting/appeal deadline, or guaranteed team SLA. Some can remain owner configuration; launch service promises require founder/team capacity.
